## How It Works

Kicker:

> How we work together

Title:

> Clear expectations before the first line of code.

Default state:

- Shows the three cards with number and title only.
- The explanatory text area starts invisible.
- Desk/chair scene is visible.

Hover/focus behavior:

- Hovering or focusing a card reveals only the related paragraph text in the right-side area.
- The card number and title are not repeated in the revealed text area.
- The desk fades out while a card is hovered/focused.
- The desk fades back in when hover/focus leaves the cards.
- The background video changes according to the hovered/focused card.

Mobile behavior:

- The cards become an accordion.
- Tapping a card opens its text inside that card.
- Tapping another card switches the open accordion item.
